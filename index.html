I still remember clearly the first day that I fell in love with you. I was ordering desserts from you when I noticed, the longer we texted each other, the more I knew that we had a lot in common. Your updates and your kindness made me fall deeply for you.

First date

I still remember our first real date, at the stadium. Back then, I had no knowledge about fashion haha. We ate a burger and I could not help but notice you ate a burger with a spoon! I will not judge you for that.
